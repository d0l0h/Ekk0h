# Changelog

## 1.1.0

### Screen saver
- Screen saver button and pop-out in the Builder, with a live preview.
- Each pack saves its own screen saver options (picture, video or theme animation, seconds, slow pan/zoom).
- Settings tab: Match Packs (plays the pack in use, with its own options) or Custom (Pack Media, Pictures, Videos or any theme's animation).
- Video screen savers with Cycle or Loop, played with the built-in player.
- Slow, GPU-smoothed pan/zoom on panorama packs; videos are never zoomed.
- Any key or mouse move now always ends the saver. Galaxy circles removed.

### Lock screen
- Lock screen is its own pop-out with a preview on the right and an explicit Save button.
- Follows the pack in use; panoramas honor the Builder's rotation.
- Settings tab: Match Packs or Custom (Windows Default or your own picture).

### Builder and Packs
- Builder always starts blank; the pack in use stays selected on the Packs tab.
- Lock screen and screen saver options are kept per pack and blank for a new pack.
- Rotate buttons swapped to match direction; Position button removed.

### Settings and interface
- Save Settings button; scrolling no longer cuts off the bottom buttons.
- Mouse wheel no longer changes drop-down lists by accident.
- Display tab renamed; Title Case and shorter hint text throughout; themed video list.
